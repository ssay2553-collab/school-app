1. ADMIN HOME / COMMAND CENTER (`/admin-dashboard`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
The central operational dashboard providing metric overviews, live alerts, unread
notification counts, and instant access grid cards across four modules:
School Ops, Finance Hub, Academic Management, and Communication.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. PROFILE & ROLE NAVIGATION:
   Step 1: Observe top header bar showing Administrator Avatar, Full Name, and "Admin" role tag.
   Step 2: To inspect account settings, tap the Profile Avatar or Settings icon in the header.
   Step 3: If you hold a dual role as an Educator/Teacher, tap the "Teacher View" button in the header to switch to the Teacher Metro Dashboard.

B. INTERACTING WITH THE EVENT STATS WIDGET:
   Step 1: Locate the "Upcoming Events" banner carousel directly below the header.
   Step 2: Swipe left or right on the widget to view upcoming holidays, mid-term breaks, and exam dates.
   Step 3: Tap "View All" or click directly on any event card to launch the full Academic Calendar screen (`/academic-calendar`).

C. NAVIGATING THE QUICK LAUNCH GRID:
   Step 1: Scroll through the four color-coded operational pillars:
           - SCHOOL OPS (Blue/Purple): Manage Users, Class Registry, Generate Code, Student Attendance, Teacher Stats, Staff Att. Setup, Staff Attendance.
           - FINANCE HUB (Green/Emerald): Fees, Other Fees, Daily Financials, Financial Summary, Expenses, Payroll.
           - ACADEMIC MGMT (Purple/Indigo): Groups, Results, Approve Score, Timetables, Term Calendar.
           - COMMUNICATION (Yellow/Rose): Announcements, Staff Chat, Parent Chat.
   Step 2: Tap any menu card to navigate immediately to that module.
   Step 3: Pay attention to red badge counters on tiles (e.g., "Staff Chat", "Parent Chat", "Approve Score") indicating unread messages or pending score edit requests.

D. REFRESHING DASHBOARD DATA:
   Step 1: Touch the screen top and pull downwards until the refresh spinner appears.
   Step 2: Release to fetch latest unread message counts, attendance alerts, and event updates.

2. MANAGE USERS (`/admin-dashboard/manage-users`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
The complete administrative console for searching, creating, editing, assigning permissions,
upgrading roles, graduating, and archiving Students, Teachers, Staff, and Parents.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. FILTERING & SEARCHING USERS:
   Step 1: Tap one of the top Role Selector tabs: "Students", "Teachers", "Staff", or "Parents".
   Step 2: To search for a specific user, tap the Search Input box and type a name, email address, phone number, or student ID.
   Step 3: For student queries, tap the "Class Filter" dropdown and select a specific class arm (e.g., "Primary 3 Gold") to narrow down results.
   Step 4: Toggle the "Show Archived" switch if you need to locate deactivated or archived accounts.

B. INSPECTING & EDITING USER PROFILES:
   Step 1: Tap any user card in the list to open the User Detail Modal.
   Step 2: Review user details (Full Name, Role, Email, Phone, Linked Children/Parents, Class Assignment, Access Status).
   Step 3: Tap "Edit Profile" button.
   Step 4: Modify fields as needed: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address.
   Step 5: Tap "Save Profile Changes" to update the database.

C. ASSIGNING TEACHER ROLES, CLASSES, & SUBJECTS:
   Step 1: Switch to the "Teachers" tab and select a teacher's card.
   Step 2: Tap "Assign Class Teacher". Select the target class arm from the dropdown list and click "Save Assignment".
   Step 3: Tap "Assign Subjects". Check the checkboxes for all subjects taught by this teacher (e.g. Mathematics, Science) and click "Save Subjects".
   Step 4: Tap "Assign Department Head" (if applicable) to designate the teacher as head of Science, Arts, or Commercial departments.

D. CONFIGURING GRANULAR PERMISSIONS MATRIX:
   Step 1: Open a Staff or Teacher user detail modal and tap "Manage Permissions".
   Step 2: Configure access rights for financial desks:
           - Feeding Dues Permission: Choose "None", "View Only", "Edit", or "Full Access".
           - Bus Transport Permission: Choose "None", "View Only", "Edit", or "Full Access".
           - Extra Classes Permission: Choose "None", "View Only", "Edit", or "Full Access".
   Step 3: Configure administrative privileges:
           - News Broadcasts: Toggle "Allowed to post news".
           - Score Corrections: Toggle "Allowed to approve score edits".
           - Teacher on Duty: Toggle "Set as Teacher on Duty" for campus oversight.
   Step 4: Tap "Save Permissions".

E. MANAGING ONBOARDING & LINK CODES:
   Step 1: To regenerate a student's or staff member's signup code, open their profile and tap "Regenerate Code".
   Step 2: To regenerate a parent link token (for connecting parents to students), tap "Regenerate Parent Link Code".
   Step 3: Tap "Copy Code" to copy the 6-character token to your clipboard, or tap "Share" to send via WhatsApp/Email directly to the parent.

F. PROMOTION, GRADUATION, & ARCHIVING:
   Step 1: To promote a student to the next class, open their profile, tap "Promote/Repeat Class", select target class, and click "Confirm Promotion".
   Step 2: To graduate an entire senior class, select the class, tap "Graduate Class", confirm the prompt, and accounts will be set to Alumnus status.
   Step 3: To deactivate an account without deleting data, tap "Archive Account".
   Step 4: To unlink a parent from a student, tap "Linked Parents", locate the parent name, and click "Unlink Parent".

G. BULK USER OPERATIONS:
   Step 1: Tap the "Select" checkbox on multiple user cards, or tap "Select All" in the bulk action bar.
   Step 2: Choose a bulk action:
           - "Bulk Clear Arrears": Clears balance debts for all selected students.
           - "Bulk Delete": Permanently purges selected accounts (requires confirmation).
           - "Bulk Import": Upload a formatted CSV/Excel file to create multiple student or teacher accounts at once.

3. CLASS REGISTRY / CREATE CLASS (`/admin-dashboard/create-class`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
Management desk to define institutional class arms, set educational categories,
map subjects, and assign classroom teachers.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. CREATING A NEW CLASS ARM:
   Step 1: Locate the "Create New Class" form at the top of the screen.
   Step 2: Tap the "Class Name" input field and enter the grade level (e.g., "Basic 4", "JSS 1", "Grade 2").
   Step 3: Tap the "Arm Designation" input field and enter the arm identifier (e.g., "Gold", "Ruby", "Blue", "A").
   Step 4: Tap the "Category" dropdown selector and choose the appropriate educational level:
           - Preschool / Nursery
           - Primary / Basic
           - Junior Secondary (JSS)
           - Senior Secondary (SSS)
   Step 5: Tap the "Class Teacher" dropdown menu and select an active teacher from the list to oversee roll call and remarks.
   Step 6: Tap the "Create Class" button. The system validates the inputs and adds the class arm to the registry.

B. MANAGING EXISTING CLASS ARMS:
   Step 1: Scroll through the "Registered Classes" list below the creation form.
   Step 2: Observe each class card showing Class Name, Category, Total Enrolled Students, Assigned Class Teacher, and Subject Count.
   Step 3: To edit a class, tap the "Edit Class" button on its card. Modify the name, arm designation, or assigned teacher, then tap "Save Changes".
   Step 4: To reassign subjects to a class arm, tap "Manage Subjects", select required subject checkboxes, and click "Save Subject Roster".
   Step 5: To archive an obsolete class arm, tap the "Archive" button and confirm.

4. GENERATE CODE (`/admin-dashboard/generate-code`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
Secure code generation engine used to onboard new students, teachers, staff, and
connect parents to student accounts.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. GENERATING REGISTRATION & LINK CODES:
   Step 1: Tap the "Code Type" dropdown menu and select the required token type:
           - Student Signup Code (For new student self-registration)
           - Staff Onboarding Code (For new teacher/staff account setup)
           - Parent Link Code (For linking parent accounts to an existing student profile)
   Step 2: If generating a student code, tap the "Target Class" dropdown and select the target class arm.
   Step 3: Tap the "Expiration Window" dropdown and select validity duration (e.g. 24 Hours, 7 Days, 30 Days, or Never Expire).
   Step 4: Enter the "Quantity of Codes" to generate (e.g., 1 for single user, or 30 for an entire new class intake).
   Step 5: Tap the "Generate Code(s)" button.

B. COPYING, SHARING, & EXPORTING CODES:
   Step 1: Review generated codes displayed in the output table.
   Step 2: To copy an individual code, tap the "Copy" icon next to the code.
   Step 3: To share a code via messaging apps, tap "Share Code", choose WhatsApp, Email, or SMS, and send to the parent/candidate.
   Step 4: To download a batch list of codes, tap "Export Codes CSV" to download an Excel-compatible list for printing.

5. STUDENT ATTENDANCE OVERVIEW (`/admin-dashboard/attendance-overview`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
Executive attendance tracking dashboard monitoring daily roll call compliance across
all classes, present/absent counts, and pending class submissions.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. SELECTING DATE & FILTERS:
   Step 1: Locate the top Date Bar. Defaults to "Today".
   Step 2: To inspect past records, tap the Calendar icon, select target date, and tap "Apply Date".
   Step 3: Tap Category Pills ("All", "Preschool", "Primary", "Secondary") to filter class lists.

B. READING ATTENDANCE SUMMARY METRICS:
   Step 1: Inspect the top summary cards:
           - Total Enrolled Students (Total active student population)
           - Total Present Today (Students marked present)
           - Total Absent Today (Students marked absent)
           - Late Arrival Count (Students arriving after official start time)
           - Unmarked Classes Count (Classes whose teachers have not submitted roll call)

C. MONITORING CLASS ROLL CALL STATUS:
   Step 1: Scroll down to the "Class Attendance Roster" section.
   Step 2: Check status indicators next to each class arm:
           - Green Badge ("Completed"): Teacher has marked and submitted roll call.
           - Red/Yellow Badge ("Pending"): Teacher has not submitted roll call today.
   Step 3: Tap on any class row to navigate directly to its detailed attendance sheet (`/admin-dashboard/attendance-details`).

D. SENDING TEACHER REMINDERS:
   Step 1: If pending classes exist, tap the red "Send Alert to Pending Teachers" button.
   Step 2: Confirm prompt. The system broadcasts an immediate push notification to all class teachers who haven't marked attendance.

8. STAFF ATTENDANCE CONFIG (`/admin-dashboard/teacher-attendance-config`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
Configuration module to establish campus GPS geofencing, allowable clock-in perimeter,
official working hours, lateness thresholds, and verification methods.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. SETTING CAMPUS GPS GEOFENCE COORDINATES:
   Step 1: If you are currently physically located at the center of the campus grounds, tap the "Use Current GPS Location" button. The app fetches device latitude and longitude automatically.
   Step 2: Alternatively, manually enter values in the "Campus Latitude" and "Campus Longitude" text fields.

B. SETTING PERIMETER RADIUS:
   Step 1: Locate the "Allowed Radius (Meters)" slider or text input.
   Step 2: Set acceptable perimeter radius (e.g., enter "100" for a 100-meter radius around the campus center). Staff attempting to clock in outside this perimeter will be blocked.

C. CONFIGURING WORKING HOURS & LATENESS THRESHOLDS:
   Step 1: Tap "Official Arrival Time" picker and set start of work day (e.g., "07:30 AM").
   Step 2: Tap "Late Threshold Time" picker and set late cutoff (e.g., "08:00 AM"). Clock-ins after this time are marked "Late" and flagged for payroll deduction.
   Step 3: Tap "Official Closing Time" picker and set end of work day (e.g., "03:30 PM").

D. ENABLING VERIFICATION METHODS & SAVING:
   Step 1: Toggle verification switches:
           - "GPS Location Check" (Mandatory location verification)
           - "QR Code Scanning" (Scan campus QR code at gate)
           - "Face Recognition Check" (Facial verification)
   Step 2: Tap the "Save Geofence Configuration" button.

11. MANAGE FEES & BILLING CENTER (`/admin-dashboard/ManageFees`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
The core bursary management console used to configure term tuition bills, record
student fee payments, grant discounts/waivers, and audit daily transactions.

STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS:

A. SELECTING SESSION, TERM, & OPERATIONAL MODE:
   Step 1: Ensure active Academic Session (e.g., "2024/2025") and Active Term (e.g., "1st Term") are selected in top header.
   Step 2: Tap mode tab:
           - "Set Term Bills" (Configure default tuition per class)
           - "Record Payments" (Process cash/online/POS payments)
           - "Manage Discounts" (Apply waivers & scholarships)
           - "Daily Transactions" (Audit today's income)

B. SETTING TERM BASE BILLS:
   Step 1: Select "Set Term Bills" mode.
   Step 2: Select target educational category or class arm (e.g. "Primary Tier").
   Step 3: Enter amounts for default fee components: Base Tuition Amount, Development Fee, Technology Fee, Sports/Activity Fee.
   Step 4: Tap "Save & Publish Term Bills". The bill is automatically assigned to all enrolled students in those classes.

C. RECORDING STUDENT PAYMENTS:
   Step 1: Select "Record Payments" mode.
   Step 2: Type student name or ID in the Search Strip, or filter by Class Arm.
   Step 3: Tap "Record Payment" on the student's fee card.
   Step 4: In the Payment Modal:
           - Enter Amount Received (e.g., "50000").
           - Select Payment Method: "Cash", "Bank Transfer", "POS", or "Cheque".
           - Enter Reference / Teller Number if bank transfer or POS.
           - Enter Name of Payer in "Received From" field.
   Step 5: Tap "Submit Payment". The system updates the student's balance, logs transaction, and displays a "Print Receipt" prompt.

D. APPLYING DISCOUNTS & SCHOLARSHIPS:
   Step 1: Select "Manage Discounts" mode.
   Step 2: Search and locate target student.
   Step 3: Tap "Apply Discount".
   Step 4: Enter Discount Amount or Percentage (e.g. "20% Sibling Waiver" or "10000 Staff Discount").
   Step 5: Select Reason for Discount from dropdown.
   Step 6: Click "Save Discount Waiver".

E. AUDITING DAILY TRANSACTIONS:
   Step 1: Select "Daily Transactions" mode.
   Step 2: Select date from calendar.
   Step 3: Review itemized table of all payments received on that day.
   Step 4: To void an erroneous payment entry, tap "Delete Transaction", enter administrative authorization password, and confirm.

   Step 4: Tap "Brand Secondary Color" picker or enter Hex Code (e.g., "#4338CA").
