1. STUDENT PORTAL HOME / EXPLORER HUB (`/student-dashboard`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
The Student Dashboard is a vibrant, easy-to-use homepage that welcomes students,
displays their Magic Code (for parent linking), highlights upcoming school events,
and provides shortcuts across three main sections: Learning Hub, My World, and Community.

KEY FEATURES & CONTROLS:
- Explorer Welcome Header: Displays student profile photo, greeting, and role tag.
- Magic Code Badge:
  * Shows student's unique parent linking code (e.g., "ED-98214").
  * Clickable Badge: Tap to copy magic code to clipboard instantly to share with parents.
  * Sibling / Family Connected Indicator: Displays "FAMILY CONNECTED!" once two parents are linked.
- Event Stats Banner: Live carousel showing upcoming holidays, term events, and exams.
- Categorized Navigation Cards:
  * LEARNING HUB: Homework, Submit Note, My Stars, Study Hub, Class Times, Weekly Topics, Coding & Robotics.
  * MY WORLD: Calendar, My Plan, Magic Notes, Attendance, Settings.
  * COMMUNITY: My Teams, News Box, Play Games.
- Unread Homework Badge: Red indicator showing pending unsubmitted assignments.

HOW TO OPERATE:
1. Tap on any card in the Learning Hub, My World, or Community grids to launch a feature.
2. Tap the "Magic Code" badge to copy your linking code and send it to your parents.
3. Pull down on the screen to refresh homework alerts and announcements.

2. HOMEWORK & ASSIGNMENTS (`/student-dashboard/assignments`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
Your homework station! Lists all homework tasks assigned by your subject teachers,
showing due dates, total points, instructions, and submission status.

KEY FEATURES & CONTROLS:
- Filter Tabs:
  * Pending (Active homework requiring submission)
  * Submitted (Homework already turned in)
  * Overdue (Past due deadline)
- Assignment Card: Shows Subject, Title, Due Date, Total Score Points, and Status Badge.
- Tap to Open: Opens complete homework instructions and attached worksheet files.
- Direct "Submit Answer" Button: Quick shortcut to upload completed work.

HOW TO OPERATE:
1. Tap "Pending" tab to view new homework.
2. Click on an assignment card to read teacher instructions and view attached files.
3. Tap "Submit Answer" to start uploading your completed work.

3. SUBMIT NOTE / UPLOAD HOMEWORK (`/student-dashboard/submit-note`)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
Upload and submit your completed homework, assignment notes, or worksheet answers
directly to your teacher.

KEY FEATURES & CONTROLS:
- Assignment Selector: Choose the assignment task you are submitting.
- Typed Text Response Box: Type your answer or notes directly into the app.
- Photo / Document Attachment Uploader:
  * Take a photo of handwritten homework using device camera.
  * Select document/PDF file from device memory.
- Submit Work Button: Sends your submission to the teacher's grading desk.

HOW TO OPERATE:
1. Select assignment from dropdown.
2. Type your answer or tap "Attach Photo/File" to upload a picture of your handwritten exercise book.
3. Click "Submit Homework". A confirmation prompt will verify successful turn-in.
